FAQ

Questions, Answered

What do I get?

Handy, MatricX PRO and MatricX Bake — every tier includes all three add-ons. The higher tiers add support and services on top.

Which Blender versions are supported?

MatricX PRO and MatricX Bake: Blender 4.5 and newer, tested on 4.5, 5.1 and 5.2.

Handy: Blender 4.0 and newer, tested on 4.0, 4.5 and 5.2.

Each add-on is one download for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Do they work together?

Yes. Model with Handy, texture with MatricX PRO, then bake with MatricX Bake — it has built-in support for MatricX PRO materials, imperfections, glass and trim sheets, and bakes any other Blender material too.
