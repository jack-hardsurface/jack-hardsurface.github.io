FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do I need UVs?

MatricX PRO: not for most materials, and never for imperfection layers. Fabrics need a UV map — MatricX creates one in a click. Trim sheets write the UVs for the faces you select.

MatricX Bake: baking writes into a UV layout, so Bake prepares it for you: Prepare UVs unwraps, and Auto-Fix Bake UVs creates a clean bake UV beside your own without replacing it.

Does Bake work with PRO?

Yes. MatricX Bake has built-in support for MatricX PRO 2.1: all 16 imperfection types, every material family, its glass and its trim sheets. It also bakes any other Blender material.

What Blender versions are supported?

MatricX PRO and MatricX Bake: Blender 4.5 and newer, tested on 4.5, 5.1 and 5.2.

Handy: Blender 4.0 and newer, tested on 4.0, 4.5 and 5.2.

Each add-on is one download for Windows, macOS and Linux.
