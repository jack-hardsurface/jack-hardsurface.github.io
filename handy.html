One menu

Every Tool, One Shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+H in the 3D View, in Object Mode or Edit Mode. The menu is ordered the way a model comes together: get into the mesh, block out shapes, cut them in, shape the topology, then repair.

Every drag tool shows its values on screen, cancels with Esc or right-click, and keeps its options in the F9 panel afterwards. Set your own hotkeys by pressing them in Preferences.

The tools

7 Tools for Clean Hard-Surface Topology

Each one fixes a step where Blender's own tools fight you.

UnBevel

Take an applied bevel back to its sharp edge — or drag to narrow or widen it.

So a bevel that came out wrong is a quick fix, not a remodel.

Smart Inset

Inset faces by an even distance without moving their outline, so the bevels and fillets beside them never crowd or shrink.

So panels and vents keep their rounded edges intact.

Offset Cut

Support loops on both sides of a Boolean seam, cut like a pipe, so dense and curved seams come out clean.

So you stop hand-cutting support loops around every seam.

Smart Loop

A loop cut that carries on through triangles and n-gons instead of stopping at them.

So you can add edge flow without cleaning up every triangle first.

Smart Primitive

Drop a cube, cylinder or sphere on the surface under the mouse, lined up with it. Click to add, Ctrl+Click to cut — the cutters stay live.

So blocking out details doesn't mean snapping and rotating every cutter by hand.

Select Seam

Select a Boolean's whole seam from a single edge.

So Boolean cleanup starts with the right edges already selected.

Smart Boolean & Safe Edit

Smart Boolean applies a mesh's Boolean modifiers, cleans up the result and shades it by angle in one click. Safe Edit applies the object's scale as you press Tab, so every tool works at the right size.

So your Booleans come out clean and every tool behaves the same, whatever the object's scale.

Also in the menu

Boolean Cleanup

Merges the seam vertices a Boolean doesn't need, on the side you choose.

Circle

Cuts a circle of edges around each selected vertex — drag for the radius, wheel for the segments.

Clean Up

Welds doubles, removes slivers and strays, fixes normals, then selects what still needs a look.
